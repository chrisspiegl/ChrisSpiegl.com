# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

This website is a personal site. It doesn't use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or third-party analytics, and it has no accounts, comments, or forms.

## Who is responsible

CSX.Ventures OÜ, Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia, is the data controller for this website. You can reach me at {% email "", "Privacy Request" %}. Full company details are in the [Legal Disclosure](/legal-disclosure/).

## Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) and delivered through Cloudflare's network. When you visit, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser, and the pages you request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This is necessary to run the website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Fonts and images are also delivered from this domain (and its subdomain r2.chrisspiegl.com) through Cloudflare, so no request goes to Google or other font or image services.

## Analytics

I use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It sets no cookies, doesn't fingerprint you, and doesn't track you across websites. I only see aggregated numbers.

## Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply and keep the conversation. I don't share it or add you to any list.

## Links and embeds

Links to other sites (YouTube, Instagram, Substack, and others) lead to services with their own privacy policies. If a page embeds a video or post from one of them, that service may receive data about your visit once the embed loads.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data I hold about you, and have it corrected, deleted, restricted, or handed over. You can also object to processing. Just email me. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate ([aki.ee](https://www.aki.ee/en)) or the data protection authority where you live.
